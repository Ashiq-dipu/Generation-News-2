HOW WE FLIPPED THE MODEL

Skills over speeches.
Outputs over certificates.

Every program we run is built around doing something real, start to finish, in a short cycle — whether that's wiring a residential breaker box, designing and reselling custom sneakers, running a mock food stall, or cutting an editorial video portfolio.

We work exclusively with mentors who practice the craft professionally — not generalist facilitators reading a script. And we keep our program list rotating, because the skills worth learning at 16 keep changing, and we'd rather stay useful than stay comfortable.

THE GENERATION METHOD

Built on four non-negotiables.

We designed these rules so young people walk away with tangible power, not just attendance marks.

01

Hands-On First

Theory takes 15 minutes max per session. The rest is hands on tools, needles, code, cameras, or pans. You learn by doing, troubleshooting, and correcting in real time.

02

Real Practitioners

Instructors work or have worked in the actual field. When an electrician teaches circuits or a streetwear designer teaches custom leather, you get real industry trade secrets.

03

Short, Focused Cycles

Programs run in 4 to 6-week sprints. High intensity, fast deliverables, and no dragged-out academic fatigue. Momentum stays high until the final product reveal.

04

Something to Keep

Every participant walks out with a physical product, a public portfolio, a registered micro-venture, or a licensed certification. Proof you can show anyone.

TRUST & INTEGRITY

Our Safeguarding Commitment

We believe hands-on trade training must be the safest place in a young person's week.

✓ 100% Background Checks All mentors undergo thorough national criminal checks and youth protection training.
✓ 1:4 Mentor Ratio No crowded classrooms. Tight ratios mean constant supervision and personalized skill coaching.
✓ Parent Check-In Desk Parents receive weekly build progress digests and are invited to all showcase events.
✓ OSHA & PPE Gear Full protective equipment provided at no cost for every electrical and workshop student.
