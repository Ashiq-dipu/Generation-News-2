Everything we've built, written & tested — free to keep.

Whether you're currently in a cohort or learning on your own, these practical guides, templates, and checklists give you a head start.

TRADES & SAFETY PDF • 14 pgs

Residential Electrical Safety & Hand Tool Checklist

Authored by Master Electrician Marcus Vance. Covers non-contact voltage testing, circuit identification, wire gauges, and essential starter tools under $100.

CREATIVE BIZ PDF • 22 pgs

Sneaker Resale & Materials Sourcing Guide

The exact prep formulas, deglazing methods, Angelus paint sealers, and margin formulas our Sneaker Lab students use to price custom kicks for 40%+ profit.

Teen Freelancer Invoice & Client Agreement Template

A plain-English 1-page service contract and invoice template designed for teens selling photos, video edits, website setups, or handyman repair work.

High School Resume & Hands-On Portfolio Pack

How to write a standout resume when you have "no prior work experience." How to list workshop projects, certifications, and technical skills effectively.

Parent & Guardian Guide to Youth Vocations

Everything parents want to know: how trade apprenticeships compare to 4-year college debt, how our safety protocols operate, and how to support your child's micro-venture.

A curated directory of 45+ local and national trade scholarships, youth entrepreneur grants, and tool allowances that high school and teen builders can apply for.
