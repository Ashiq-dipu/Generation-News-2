PARENT & GUARDIAN CORNER

What to expect when your teen joins Generation News.

We know entrusting your teenager's time and safety is a big decision. Here is our direct promise to every parent:

  • ✓ Complete Safety First: All bays are supervised by licensed professionals. Safety gear, eye protection, and gloves are provided and mandatory.
  • ✓ Zero Financial Pressure: Every young person who qualifies for financial need receives 100% tuition coverage plus materials.
  • ✓ Tangible Confidence: You will see your teen bring home things they wired, designed, or prepared within the very first 3 weeks.
