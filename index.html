ABOUT OUR ORIGIN

We got tired of programs that teach youth about opportunity instead of handing them one.

Generation News began with a simple observation: most youth programs are built around talking — motivational sessions, career day speeches, worksheets about "finding your passion." Good intentions, weak results.

So we flipped it. Every program we run is built around doing something real, start to finish, in a short cycle — whether that's learning basic electrical wiring, designing and reselling custom sneakers, running a mock food stall, or building a first portfolio in digital content. Young people leave with a skill, a product, or a small win they can point to.

OUR MISSION

Every young person deserves one real skill before they turn 20.

Generation News exists to close the gap between youth potential and youth opportunity by delivering hands-on, mentor-led skill development programs — trades, creative business, digital, and life skills — built for ages 13–20, designed to end in something real: a product, a portfolio, or a proven ability.

#1 Skill over ceremony

No program graduates without a tangible outcome.

#2 Access over barriers

Scholarships and free cohorts ensure cost is never an issue.

#3 Mentors over lectures

Every instructor is an active professional in the trade.

#4 Relevance over routine

We build programs around skills that matter in the real economy today.
