OUR CORE PURPOSE ● Tangible Skill Equity

Every young person deserves one real skill before they turn 20.

Not just advice about the future. Not a flyer about career choices. A tangible ability they can hold in their hands, build with, repair with, and earn with.

The Generation News Mission Statement

"Generation News exists to close the gap between youth potential and youth opportunity by delivering hands-on, mentor-led skill development programs — trades, creative business, digital, and life skills — built for ages 13–20, designed to end in something real: a product, a portfolio, or a proven ability."

HOW WE OPERATE

The Four Commitments

We promise every student, parent, and donor that these standards are strictly upheld.

01 NO EMPTY CEREMONY

Skill Over Ceremony

No participant graduates from Generation News just by sitting in a chair for 40 hours. Graduation happens when you wire a live breaker box, sell a pair of sneakers you customized, or present a viable food business model to real patrons.

02 ZERO COST ROADBLOCKS

Access Over Cost Barriers

A teenager should never be locked out of learning a trade because their family can't afford $800 in workshop fees or specialized tools. 100% of our need-based applicants receive full zero-cost scholarships and protective gear.

03 PRACTITIONERS ONLY

Mentors Over Lectures

Every single mentor in our bays works or has worked commercially in the exact field they instruct. You are learning directly from licensed electricians, active brand founders, executive chefs, and professional media producers.

04 STAYING RELEVANT

Relevance Over Routine

We retire programs the moment market demands shift. If a tool or process becomes obsolete, we update our curriculum within 30 days. We would rather stay useful to a teenager's immediate future than stay comfortable with old syllabi.
